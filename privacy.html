Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Crafters Publisher is a private tool run by one person (the channel owner) on their own computer. It signs in only to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook and TikTok accounts to upload videos the owner made.

What it accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (youtube.upload).
  • Facebook: permission to publish videos to the owner's Page.
  • TikTok: basic profile info and permission to publish videos.

What it stores

Only the sign-in tokens, kept in a local file on the owner's computer. No personal data about anyone else is collected, sold or shared. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms' own APIs.

How to revoke

Access can be removed at any time in your Google account (Security > Third-party access), Facebook settings (Business integrations) or TikTok settings (Manage app permissions).
